# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-20)

- First release. The Wisplight can be upgraded to level 5 by default. Each level widens the area your wisp clears of mist, from the vanilla 15 m at level 1 to 350 m at level 5, and thins more of the Mistlands fog. From level 2 mist inside that area disappears at once. Every upgrade costs wisps, plus silver for levels 2 and 3, soft tissue for level 3, Eitr for level 4 and a majestic carapace from the Seeker Queen for level 5. The Wisplight's tooltip shows what its level does and what the next level brings. By default a Wisplight anywhere in your inventory gives you its wisp without taking the utility slot; turn on `EquipRequired` to need it equipped. It also works alongside RossQoL's WisplightCarry.
