# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-20)

- First release. The Wisplight can be upgraded to level 5 by default. Each level widens the area your wisp clears of mist, from the vanilla 15 m at level 1 to 350 m at level 5, and thins more of the Mistlands fog. From level 2 mist inside that area disappears at once. Upgrades cost wisps and silver up to level 3, then wisps and Eitr. By default a Wisplight anywhere in your inventory gives you its wisp without taking the utility slot; turn on `EquipRequired` to need it equipped. It also works alongside RossQoL's WisplightCarry.
