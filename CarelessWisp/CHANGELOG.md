# Changelog

- Clearer description and readme. No change to how the mod works.

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-20)

- First release. The Wisplight can be upgraded to level 5 by default. Each level widens the area your wisp clears of mist, from the vanilla 15 m at level 1 to 350 m at level 5, and thins more of the Mistlands fog. From level 2, the cleared area is centred on you rather than on the wandering wisp, and mist inside it fades out in about 1.5 seconds instead of lingering for up to 5. Every upgrade costs wisps, plus silver for levels 2 and 3, soft tissue for level 3, Eitr for level 4 and a majestic carapace from the Seeker Queen for level 5. The Wisplight's tooltip shows what its level does and what the next level brings. By default a Wisplight anywhere in your inventory gives you its wisp without taking the utility slot; turn on `EquipRequired` to need it equipped. It also works alongside RossQoL's WisplightCarry.
