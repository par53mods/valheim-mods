# Changelog

## 1.0.5 (2026-11-09)

- New Keep settings, all off by default, to hold items back from the chests: `PreservePortalMats`, `PreserveBoatMats` (the best boat you've learned and can build), `PreserveBossItems` (only when you carry exactly what an altar takes), `KeepOutpostKit` (workbench, campfire and bed), `KeepCoins`, `KeepSpareAmmo` and a `KeepItems` list such as `Wood:20, Resin:10`.
- When something is held back, the message says so. Pressing Quick Stack again stacks it too, until you close the inventory.

## 1.0.4 (2026-11-08)

- Choose which kinds of items Quick Stack sends to chests: `StackMaterials` and `StackRawFood` are on by default, `StackCookedFood` and `StackMeads` are off, so cooked food and meads stay with you.
- If a chest answers late, the message updates to match what went in, instead of wrongly saying there was no room.

## 1.0.3 (2026-11-06)

- Quick Stack says what it stacked, with amounts, and names anything that had no room, in smaller centre-screen text.

## 1.0.2 (2026-11-06)

- Reports which items had no room when chests are full, e.g. "No room to stack carrots, fine wood".
- One message once every chest has answered, instead of one per chest.
- New description.

## 1.0.1 (2026-11-03)

- The Quick Stack button sits a quarter of its height lower.

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-03)

- First release: an inventory button that moves your stackable items into nearby chests already holding them.
