# Changelog

## 1.0.2 (2026-11-06)

- Necks turn violet, along with their neck tails.
- Boar leather scraps, wolf pelts, bear hides and the wolf and bear rugs take their animal's colour.

## 1.0.1 (2026-11-06)

- Paints boars pink, bears bright green and wolves turquoise, including their young and corpses.
- New settings `BoarColor`, `BearColor` and `WolfColor` (red, green, blue, each from 0 to 255).

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-03)

- First release: recolours deer, deer hides and the deer rug, and turns crows gold, in colours you choose.
